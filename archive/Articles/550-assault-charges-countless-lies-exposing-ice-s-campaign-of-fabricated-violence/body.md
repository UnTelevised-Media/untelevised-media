Of the more than 400 cases that have been resolved, **nearly half resulted in charges being thrown out, withdrawn, or ending in acquittal.** Federal agents engaged in extensive misconduct: attacking protesters, destroying evidence, misrepresenting facts in court.

[The same day the investigation was published, we learned that two more people had been killed by ICE agents in less than a week.](https://untelevised.media/articles/ice-kills-again-two-fatal-shootings-halt-overturned/) Neither was an *"intended target."* Neither was armed. Neither posed a threat that required lethal force.

And then came the capper: ICE agents were told that the **FBI would no longer investigate confrontations involving immigration agents** — meaning the agency that has been killing civilians with impunity would now investigate itself.

This is the machinery of state violence in its purest form: manufacture the crime, destroy the evidence, eliminate the investigators, and blame the victims.

## The 550 Cases: A Mass Prosecution Machine Built on Lies

The Times investigation reveals the anatomy of a prosecutorial fraud:

**The Scale.** More than 550 assault-on-federal-officer cases filed against people ensnared in immigration enforcement. This is not a few bad apples. This is an industrial operation.
**The Collapse.** Of 400+ resolved cases, nearly half — roughly 200 — fell apart. Charges were dismissed. Evidence was insufficient. Juries acquitted. Judges threw cases out.

## The FBI Blackout: Eliminating the Last Check

The decision to pull the FBI from investigations of ICE confrontations is the single most damning development in this story — because it reveals what the administration fears most: *evidence.*

FBI investigations of ICE confrontations have, in the past, gathered evidence that *implicated agents themselves* — evidence that could support civil rights prosecutions, internal discipline, or public accountability. The FBI is not a civil rights organization. But its involvement created at least the possibility that an agent who fabricated an assault claim might face consequences.

That possibility is now gone.

Under the new guidance, Homeland Security Investigations — a unit within ICE itself — will examine incidents in which an agent claims to have been assaulted. HSI does not have jurisdiction to investigate civil rights violations. In practice, this means that if an ICE agent kills someone and claims self-defense, the investigation will be conducted by the agent's own agency, by investigators who cannot legally examine whether the agent violated the victim's civil rights.

Aaron Reichlin-Melnick, a senior fellow at the American Immigration Council, called it what it is: *"The fox is guarding the hen house."*

The DOJ and DHS released a joint statement denying the change would take place — even as agents across the country received guidance telling them exactly the opposite. The contradiction speaks for itself.

## The Arrest Surge: 1,400 Per Day

While the assault cases collapse and the investigations are shuttered, ICE is accelerating its dragnet. The Washington Post reported on July 17 that ICE has arrested an average of **more than 1,400 people per day in July** — the highest number under the Trump administration.

More than 10,000 people detained in the first five days of July alone. Families torn apart. Workers pulled from job sites. Students taken from classrooms.

And where do they go? Into the detention system that we have [exposed as a profit machine](https://untelevised.media/articles/immigration-detention-system-expansion-2026/)[ for private prison corporations. GEO Group and CoreCivic — the same companies that have ](https://untelevised.media/articles/immigration-detention-system-expansion-2026/)[built a $529 million detention center in Colorado](https://untelevised.media/articles/ice-529-million-colorado-detention-center-geo-group/) — are processing human beings at record volume.

The pipeline is simple: arrest → charge → detain → bill the taxpayer. The assault charges serve a dual purpose: they justify the arrests to a public that might otherwise question why 1,400 people per day are being disappeared, and they create leverage — defendants facing federal assault charges are more likely to accept deportation rather than fight.

## The Architecture of Impunity

What we are witnessing is not a series of separate scandals. It is a coordinated campaign to immunize a federal law enforcement agency from accountability:

  1. **Fabricate assault charges** against people caught in the dragnet, creating a narrative that agents are under siege
  1. **Destroy evidence** in the cases that go to court, ensuring the fabricated narrative survives judicial scrutiny
  1. **Pull the FBI from investigations**, eliminating the only external entity capable of gathering evidence against agents
  1. **Surge arrests** to 1,400 per day, overwhelming the system and creating a volume problem that makes individual accountability impossible
  1. **Kill with impunity**, knowing that the investigative mechanism has been dismantled

Each step reinforces the others. The fabricated assault charges justify the removal of oversight. The removal of oversight enables more fabricated charges. The surge in arrests makes the individual cases impossible to track. The killings continue.
