## The Network: From California Riots to Tennessee Gyms

The Active Club network was founded by Robert Rundo, a self-identified fascist and white nationalist who launched the movement in 2021 while hiding out in Serbia to avoid federal charges for violently assaulting protesters at political rallies across California. Rundo, a former member of the Rise Above Movement (RAM), was caught on video attacking a Trump protester at a 2017 California rally. In December 2024, he was sentenced to 24 months in federal prison after pleading guilty to conspiracy to riot — a charge stemming from organized violence at rallies in Huntington Beach, Berkeley, San Bernardino, and Charlottesville.

The U.S. Department of Justice documented Rundo's role in what it called *"planning and engaging in riots at political rallies across California."* He was also convicted of gang assault in New York at age 19, according to sentencing memos. After his release from federal prison — time served — Rundo was placed on parole with conditions requiring periodic monitoring of his electronic devices.

But the movement he built didn't stop growing. The Southern Poverty Law Center reports that Active Clubs expanded from 12 chapters in 2022 to 42 by 2025. And Tennessee sits at the center of the network's operations in the American South.

Rundo has described the fight exhibitions his followers participate in as a *"tip-off point for a fascist cultural revolution."* He added: *"Every victorious nationalist movement was forged by hardened men who already tasted real danger."*

## Who Runs the Tennessee Active Club

The Tennessee Active Club is run by Sean Kauffmann, a figure described by the Stop Antisemitism watchdog group as *"a disturbed neo-Nazi and Holocaust denier with a documented history of violence and a massive cache of firearms."*

In March 2024, he was arrested again — this time for allegedly assaulting his female partner by *"punching her numerous times in the face and head, while she was holding their infant son,"* according to the Perry County Sheriff's Office arrest report. The case was documented through a public records request filed by journalist Eric Levai via MuckRock.

Kauffmann is currently sitting in a Perry County jail, awaiting trial on a domestic violence charge. His bond was revoked in December 2025 after he gave NewsChannel 5's camera the middle finger in open court — an act of contempt that the judge found warranted revoking his freedom.

But Kauffmann's influence extends far beyond his jail cell. In audio messages obtained by the SPLC, Kauffmann confirmed his dual membership in both the Active Clubs and Patriot Front, stating: *"I have worked heavily with Patriot Front. I have been part of Patriot Front."* A leaked internal report from Patriot Front describes the Tennessee Active Club as *"ran by member. Heavily cooperative"* — confirming that Kauffmann's operation is essentially a subsidiary recruitment arm of the larger white nationalist organization.

Under Kauffmann's leadership, the Tennessee Active Club has organized networking events, fight clubs, and propaganda campaigns across the state. His mother was even photographed wearing a Tennessee Active Club shirt, which the group shared on its Telegram channel in January 2023.

Now Rettke has resurfaced in the Active Club scene. ## Where They Operate: The Tennessee Infrastructure

The Tennessee Active Club has operated out of multiple locations across the state:

**Lewis Country Store (Nashville area):** A Second Amendment-themed gas station and general store owned by Brad Lewis, who has described himself as *"an actual literal Nazi."* The SPLC revealed in 2023 that the second floor of the store was being used as a *"white nationalist fight club"* by the Tennessee Active Club. The store became a gathering point for fight training and networking events. Lewis told Rolling Stone that the Active Club members were *"friends."*
**Shelbyville BJJ Academy (Shelbyville):** In June 2025, the Guardian revealed that the South Central Tennessee Active Club had been secretly using this Brazilian jiu-jitsu school for combat training — a facility that offers classes to children as young as three. Video posted to Telegram showed Active Club members training at the academy with a sonnenrad flag — an ancient symbol co-opted by Nazi Germany — hanging on the wall. One participant, William Chase May, was identified by the Guardian through Smoothcomp competition records showing he received his blue belt from the academy in 2023. After the Guardian's inquiry, the academy confirmed May had been banned and changed keypad access codes. The school stated: *"That type of behavior at our gym is a direct violation of our code of ethics."*
**Patriot Front Compound (Tellico Plains):** A 122-acre property in East Tennessee, located between Chattanooga and Knoxville, serves as a training compound operated by Patriot Front. The facility is managed by Ian Michael Elliott, a longtime Patriot Front leader who also serves as bodyguard for the group's founder, Thomas Rousseau. CNN reported in December 2025 that the building where Elliott conducted training had been put up for sale, though Patriot Front intends to continue developing the surrounding land into a larger compound. The facility has hosted combat training events and grapple tournaments involving Active Club members from across the country.
**Commercial Gyms (Nashville area):** The Active Club has also used commercial jujitsu gyms near Armory Drive in Nashville. Photos show Patriot Front recruiter Ian Elliott training at the same facility where Rettke worked out.

## The Patriot Front Connection: The Hidden Hand

The most significant revelation about the Tennessee Active Club is who actually controls it. In May 2025, the SPLC published a bombshell report based on leaked internal documents and audio messages revealing that Patriot Front — the most active white nationalist group in the United States — is the "hidden hand" behind approximately a dozen Active Clubs across the country, including the Tennessee chapter.

Patriot Front, led by Thomas Rousseau, operates from a command center in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. The group was responsible for producing the majority of white supremacist propaganda in the U.S. in recent years, according to researchers. In 2024 alone, Patriot Front members posted more than 5,000 pieces of racist propaganda across the country — 10 times more than any other group, according to the SPLC's hate flyering map.

The leaked report categorized Active Clubs into tiers: "subsidiary recruiting projects" directly controlled by Patriot Front, "cooperative independent clubs," and "non-cooperative" chapters. The Tennessee Active Club fell into the most entwined category — described as "ran by member. Heavily cooperative."

Financial ties between the two organizations run deep. A leaked banking document obtained by the nonprofit media outlet Unicorn Riot revealed that a Patriot Front inner-circle member, Graham Whitson, established a bank account for Will2Rise — a white nationalist clothing brand founded by Rundo and Grady Mayfield — with a projected monthly income of $10,000. Whitson is Rousseau's former roommate and Patriot Front's chief propagandist.
