## ☠️ Selling Mississippi to Musk: The xAI Disaster

Tate Reeves' relationship with Elon Musk might be the most egregious example of his sell-out of Mississippi.

### The $20 Billion Deal

Elon Musk's artificial intelligence startup xAI has invested **more than $20 billion** to build a data center in Southaven, Mississippi — with the **"full backing" of Governor Tate Reeves**.

The facility powers xAI's Grok chatbot and is part of the "Colossus" data center network.

But the facility came with a price tag that Mississippi is paying in **clean air and public health**.

### The Lawsuit

The NAACP, represented by Earthjustice and the Southern Environmental Law Center, **sued xAI and subsidiary MZX Tech in April 2026**, charging they violated the federal Clean Air Act by running **27 gas-fired turbines** before getting necessary air permits.

### The Pollution

The potential emissions are **staggering**:

| Pollutant | Annual Emissions | Health Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nitrogen oxides (NOx) | 1,700 tons | Smog, respiratory issues |
| Fine particulate matter | 180 tons | Heart disease, lung cancer |
| Carbon monoxide | 500 tons | Headaches, dizziness |
| Formaldehyde | 19 tons | Cancer-causing |

### The Environmental Racism

These emissions are pouring into communities near Memphis, Tennessee, and Southaven, Mississippi — **predominantly Black neighborhoods** that are already facing environmental justice challenges.

> "By looking to evade clean air laws to operate dirty turbines that emit pollution and known carcinogens, these companies are following a shameful, familiar pattern: asking Black and frontline communities to bear the toxic brunt of 'innovation,'" said **Abre' Conner**, director of the Center for Environmental and Climate Justice at the NAACP.

### The Rubber Stamp

Mississippi regulators held **one public hearing** in February 2026 about permits for those turbines after **just a few days of public notice**, then approved the permits anyway.

Residents have complained about noise and pollution from the facility. The area around Southaven has faced increased air quality concerns, with local residents reporting respiratory issues and reduced quality of life.

## 🏥 The Medicaid Betrayal: Blocking Healthcare for the Poorest

Since becoming governor in 2020, Tate Reeves has **blocked Medicaid expansion for more than a decade** — despite:

- ✅ Bipartisan support in the Mississippi Legislature
- ✅ Overwhelming evidence that expansion would save lives and create jobs
- ✅ 90% federal funding

In March 2024, Reeves summoned state senators to the Governor's Mansion and **privately told them he would veto any Medicaid expansion bill** sent to his desk in 2024.

> This isn't fiscal conservatism. This is cruelty.

### The Coverage Gap

Mississippi is one of the **last states that hasn't expanded Medicaid** under the Affordable Care Act. As a result, roughly **100,000 Mississippi adults** fall into a "**coverage gap**":

- They earn **too much** to qualify for traditional Medicaid
- They earn **too little** to qualify for subsidies on the ACA marketplace

These are working people. They're the people who:

- 🍽️ Serve your food
- 🧹 Clean your offices
- 👶 Take care of your children

They're the people who make Mississippi run, but they **can't afford to see a doctor when they're sick**.

### The Math

Medicaid expansion would bring:

- 💰 **Billions in federal dollars** to Mississippi

- 🏥 **Save rural hospitals** on the brink of closure

It's a no-brainer.

But Tate Reeves has said **no for more than a decade**.

His stated reason is that Mississippi can't afford the state's share of the costs.
