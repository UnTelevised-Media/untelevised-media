## Argentina: The Old Familiar Refuge

And then there's the destination itself. Because you don't accidentally pick Argentina when you're a Silicon Valley billionaire with a passport collection that already includes New Zealand and Malta.

Argentina holds a particular place in the history of men like Thiel. After World War II, the Perón regime collaborated with Croatian fascist priest Krunoslav Draganović's "ratlines" — escape routes that smuggled as many as 5,000 Nazi war criminals out of Europe and into South America. The regime funneled at least 1 million Swiss francs into operating additional ratlines through Scandinavia and Switzerland. Josef Mengele. Adolf Eichmann. Erich Priebke. Some of the worst monsters of the 20th century found sanctuary in the very country where Thiel is now buying real estate.

This isn't ancient history with no modern resonance. The ideological descendants of the men who fled to Argentina after Nuremberg are now in power — and they're the same people Thiel has spent his career cultivating.
