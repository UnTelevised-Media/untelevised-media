In a stunning federal court filing last week, the Trump administration made an admission that would have triggered impeachment proceedings in any previous era: it explicitly confirmed that it canceled **$7.5 billion in clean energy grants** to states that voted for Kamala Harris in 2024, while leaving identical grants in Republican states untouched. The cancellation affected **284 projects across 16 states and territories** — California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Hawaii, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, Oregon, Vermont, and Washington — all of them places that went blue in November.

*"With one exception, the 284 terminated grants had a recipient location and/or at least one place of performance in a state that awarded its electoral votes to Kamala Harris in the 2024 election and has two Democratic-caucusing senators,"* a Department of Energy attorney wrote in the filing. The grants, the government conceded, were not terminated *"based on any programmatic, statutory, cost-reduction or performance-based factor."*

They were terminated, the filing acknowledged, *"based solely on the political identity of the grant recipient's state."*

This is not a scandal. This is the operating system of the Trump administration — laid bare, in its own handwriting, in federal court.

## The Pattern: Federal Funds as Political Weapon

The $7.5 billion in energy grants is not an isolated incident. It is one front in a systematic campaign to weaponize the entire apparatus of federal spending against states that didn't support Trump — a campaign that spans disaster relief, childcare funding, university research, and infrastructure investment. As UnTelevised has documented, this weaponization extends into virtually every arm of federal power: [Trump pardoned corrupt officials and dismantled the anti-corruption oversight office](https://untelevised.media/articles/trump-pardons-corrupt-officials-dismantles-anti-corruption-oversight-office/) meant to keep his administration in check, clearing the path for exactly this kind of unchecked abuse.

**Energy Grants ($7.5 billion terminated):**
- 284 clean energy projects canceled in October 2025
- Targeted: 16 states + DC that voted for Harris
- Spared: Identical projects in Republican-voting states, despite DOE recommending their cancellation
- White House dubbed the money *"Green New Scam funding to fuel the Left's climate agenda"*
- Trump shared video of OMB Director Russ Vought as the Grim Reaper, soundtrack: *"gonna tie your hands"*
- Energy Secretary Chris Wright testified one month ago that politics played no role: *"No decisions were made on politics... It's bullshit. We're going to say it a million times."* His own lawyers contradicted him in court a week later.

- Agencies *"did not terminate any grants based on alleged noncompliance with the terms of the grant"*
- Instead used *"general criteria, rather than grant-specific assessment"*
- UC researchers' lawsuit (Thakur v. Trump) argues three constitutional violations: First Amendment viewpoint discrimination, punishment based on state residency, and failure to spend money as Congress directed
- Judge Rita F. Lin has repeatedly sided with UC researchers and issued preliminary injunctions restoring grants

**FEMA Disaster Aid (pattern of denials):**

- Also denied aid to **Colorado and California** around the same time
- July 2026: Denied $227 million in blizzard recovery aid to **New York, New Jersey, Massachusetts, and Rhode Island** — all four states exceeded FEMA's damage threshold
- Two days before denying those four blue states, Trump **approved six red states** for disaster aid

- Rhode Island's congressional delegation: *"Trump has politicized disaster assistance... made it exponentially harder for so-called 'blue states' to get disaster funding"*
- Gov. Kathy Hochul: *"New Yorkers... deserve access to every resource available. Instead they have a President who is turning his back on his home state."*

## The Architecture of Retribution

Russ Vought, the Project 2025 architect who now runs the White House Office of Management and Budget, is the architect of this system. Vought has publicly called federal grants to blue states *"taxpayer funding for the Left"* and has built an infrastructure designed to ensure that federal money flows only to places that voted correctly.

The White House is currently pushing a proposed rule to **give political appointees direct authority over federal grant awards**, replacing the current expert-led, peer-review system used in science and defense research. Heather Pierce, senior director of science policy at the Association of American Medical Colleges, told NPR that the rule *"would essentially undermine that process by adding a layer of political review that could undo scientific recommendations for reasons that have nothing to do with science."*

This is the point. The court filing admitting to political retaliation is not a mistake — it is a declaration of intent. The Trump administration wants political control over federal spending codified as official policy. The lawsuits are obstacles. The proposed rule is the endgame.

Democratic lawmakers responded to the court filing with characteristically muted outrage. *"This Administration has now admitted in court what has long been obvious: it terminated nearly 300 cost-cutting energy projects for no reason other than the fact that the states they were in did not vote for the President,"* House Democrats wrote in a statement. *"This is an astounding admission that the President and his team corruptly abused their power to kill good jobs and punish hard-working families because of their political views."*

*Astounding* is one word for it. *Corrupt* is another. *Astounding* suggests surprise. There is nothing surprising about a president who campaigned on retribution delivering retribution.

## What This Means

The admission in federal court is significant not because it reveals something new, but because it confirms something the administration has been doing in plain sight since January 2025. Federal disaster relief is being denied to blue states while red states receive it. Federal research grants are being terminated based on keyword searches designed to target topics the White House finds ideologically objectionable. Federal infrastructure funding is being redirected based on electoral maps. And the administration is pushing to make this political control of federal spending permanent.

The $7.5 billion in canceled energy grants went to projects designed to reduce energy costs, create jobs, and transition the grid — projects in both red and blue states, funded by legislation passed by Congress. The Trump administration killed the blue-state projects and kept the red-state ones. It then admitted in court that the decision was *"based solely"* on politics. Energy Secretary Chris Wright lied to Congress about it. Russ Vought choreographed it. Trump celebrated it.

This is not governance. This is organized extortion of the American people through the control of their own tax dollars. Your money is being withheld from your community because of how your state voted. The disaster relief you paid for is being given to someone else. The research grants your university earned are being burned because a keyword triggered a political flag. And the people doing it are not hiding — they are admitting it in court filings and bragging about it on social media.

The question is not whether this is unconstitutional. It plainly is — the UC lawyers have laid out three separate constitutional theories, and a federal judge has already repeatedly sided with them. The question is whether any institutional mechanism remains capable of stopping it. So far, the answer is: not Congress, not the courts at speed, and certainly not the Democratic Party, which continues to issue *"astounding"* press statements while the administration burns the country down around them.

And it flows toward the machinery of enforcement and punishment: ICE detention contracts, private prison expansions, and a Justice Department that [has been weaponized against the press](https://untelevised.media/articles/the-corrupt-core-how-trumps-second-presidency-has-weaponized-the-justice-department-against-the-press/) to silence critical coverage.
