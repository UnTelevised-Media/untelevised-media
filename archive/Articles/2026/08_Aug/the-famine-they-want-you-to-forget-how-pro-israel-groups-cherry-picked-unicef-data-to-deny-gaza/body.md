## The Source They Cited Doesn't Say What They Claim

The 0.2% to 0.8% figure is real. It exists. But here's what the Atlanta Israel Coalition doesn't tell you: **that number comes from a single Nutrition Cluster survey conducted in mid-2026, after a ceasefire had been in place for nearly ten months, and after aid flows into Gaza had partially recovered.** The survey was coordinated under the UNICEF-led Nutrition Cluster and measured child malnutrition using Weight-for-Height Z-scores (WHZ), a standard WHO methodology.

## The Numbers They Hide: Gaza's Famine Was Official, Measured, and Deadly

The Atlanta Israel Coalition wants you to believe that because malnutrition rates *currently* measure low, famine never happened. This is not how famine works. Famine is not a permanent state — it is an event, a period of acute deprivation that kills people, and then, if conditions change, it recedes. The question is not *"what are malnutrition rates today?"* The question is *"did famine occur, and who caused it?"*

On that question, the record is unambiguous.

**On August 22, 2025, the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) — a 21-agency partnership including UNICEF, the World Food Programme, the World Health Organization, and the Food and Agriculture Organization — officially confirmed famine in Gaza Governorate.** It was the first time famine had been formally declared in the Middle East.

The IPC report, a Special Snapshot covering July–September 2025, documented the following:

- **More than 640,000 people** facing Catastrophic food insecurity (IPC Phase 5) across the Strip
- **1.14 million additional people** in Emergency conditions (IPC Phase 4)
- **396,000 more** in Crisis (IPC Phase 3)
- **132,000 children under five** expected to suffer acute malnutrition — **double** the May 2025 estimate
- **Over 41,000 children** suffering severe acute malnutrition (SAM), the deadliest form, carrying both short and long-term risk of death
- **55,500 malnourished pregnant and breastfeeding women**
- **One in five babies** born prematurely or underweight

In July 2025 alone, **more than 12,000 children** were identified as acutely malnourished — the highest monthly figure ever recorded in Gaza, a six-fold increase since the start of the year. Nearly one in four of those children were suffering from severe acute malnutrition.

In the Gaza City Governorate specifically, **over 15% of children were acutely malnourished** by the end of July 2025 — the threshold the IPC uses to formally classify famine. This figure comes from a UNRWA study published in *The Lancet* in October 2025, at a time when Israel was conducting [the disarmament theater that killed 50 people in five days](https://untelevised.media/articles/the-disarmament-theater-50-killed-in-five-days-as-israel-bombs-gaza-while-the-world-negotiates/).

> UNICEF's own Executive Director, **Catherine Russell**, stated at the time: *"Famine is now a grim reality for children in Gaza Governorate, and a looming threat in Deir al-Balah and Khan Younis. As we have repeatedly warned, the signs were unmistakable: children with wasted bodies, too weak to cry or eat; babies dying from hunger and preventable disease; parents arriving at clinics with nothing left to feed their children."*

> WHO Director-General **Dr. Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus** said: *"The world has waited too long, watching tragic and unnecessary deaths mount from this man-made famine. Widespread malnutrition means that even common and usually mild diseases like diarrhoea are becoming fatal, especially for children."*

> UN Relief Chief **Tom Fletcher** was blunter: *"It is a famine, the Gaza famine."*
