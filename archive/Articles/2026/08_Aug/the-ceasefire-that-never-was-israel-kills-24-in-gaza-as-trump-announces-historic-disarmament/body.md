Two days after Donald Trump announced what he called a *"historic"* agreement for the [complete disarmament](https://untelevised.media/articles/the-disarmament-theater-50-killed-in-five-days-as-israel-bombs-gaza-while-the-world-negotiates/) of Hamas in Gaza, Israeli forces killed at least 24 Palestinians across the strip in 48 hours — including an eight-year-old girl, an 18-month-old baby, a pregnant woman, and her unborn child.

The dead were not combatants. They were families in their homes, children in tents, a couple in their sixties sheltering in the room that used to be their kitchen. The *"ceasefire"* that took effect on October 10, 2025 — already a grim misnomer given the 1,250 Palestinians killed since its signing — has become, in the space of one weekend, something worse than a euphemism. It has become a cover.

## The Disarmament Theater

On July 30, Trump took to Truth Social to declare victory. *"Today, the Board of Peace reached a HISTORIC agreement for the COMPLETE DISARMAMENT of Hamas and all other armed groups in Gaza,"* he wrote, framing the deal as the long-awaited entry into the second phase of the ceasefire framework. The BBC, CNN, and Axios amplified the announcement with varying degrees of credulity. Axios called it a *"breakthrough."* CNN called it *"historic."*

Hamas negotiator Ghazi Hamad offered the first reality check: the group would not implement any deal unless Israel halted its attacks and withdrew its forces — obligations Israel has ignored for ten months. Netanyahu's office responded that Israeli troops would not withdraw until Hamas was fully disarmed, a circular condition designed to ensure neither side ever moves.

The fundamental deadlock was unchanged. What changed was the optics. And in Gaza, the bombs did not stop.

## The Dead of July 30

The same day Trump announced *"peace,"* Israeli aircraft struck a tent encampment for displaced families in Khan Younis. A man and an eight-year-old child were killed. The camp was one of thousands of improvised shelters stretching across southern Gaza — home to people whose actual homes were destroyed by Israel's siege, which has leveled the houses of more than 1.2 million Palestinians, nearly 60 percent of the population.

In the Bureij refugee camp in central Gaza, an airstrike hit a house, killing an 18-month-old baby. Eight others were wounded. The Israeli military said both strikes targeted Hamas militants. They always say that.

In Gaza City, a man riding a bicycle was killed in a strike that wounded ten others. Al Jazeera's Hind Khoudary reported from the aftermath of a separate attack in the Shati refugee camp: *"This building was surrounded by two makeshift tent camps and these tents are now all damaged. We're talking about 35 families that were terrified when these massive explosions took place."*

## The Weekend Slaughter

Two days later, on August 2, the killing accelerated. Eighteen Palestinians were killed in a single day — among them three children and an unborn baby recovered from the rubble of a destroyed apartment building.

In Gaza City's al-Sousi Tower, a strike killed **Abdullah Abu Taif, 33**, his pregnant wife, **Abeer Anan, 29**, and their five-year-old son, **Azzam**. Medics at al-Shifa Hospital recovered the remains of Abeer's unborn child from the concrete. An entire family — four lives, one bomb.

Near Khan Younis in al-Qarara, another apartment strike killed **Mahmoud al-Hams, 38**, his wife, **Fatima, 37**, and their young daughter. Three others were wounded. Near Deir el-Balah, **Kamal Abu Muailiq, 68**, and his wife, **Huda, 59**, were killed in their home by an Israeli helicopter strike. Two brothers riding a motorcycle were killed in the same area. In Gaza City's Remal neighbourhood, a strike on a tent sheltering displaced people killed two more, including a child.

A drone strike near the Sharia Court killed four additional Palestinians. Israel said it hit military operatives. In the Jabalia refugee camp, another Palestinian was killed after being directly targeted.

On August 1, the day before the slaughter in al-Sousi Tower, Gaza's Civil Defence completed a two-week search of a flattened home in the Sabra neighbourhood. They retrieved **112 bodies. Forty of them were children.** One hundred fifty-seven people remained missing. Some, rescue worker Mohammed Abu Dan told reporters, were fused into concrete by the force of the explosions.

## The Medicine Warehouse

The killing was not limited to people. On the night of August 1, Israeli strikes destroyed the medicine warehouse attached to Al-Aqsa Hospital in Deir el-Balah — wiping out two of four storage facilities and severely damaging two more. The Israeli military claimed it hit a Hamas weapons depot next to the hospital.

Gaza's Minister of Health Director-General Munir al-Bursh called it what it was: an act that *"turns medicine and illness itself into a tool of war."* Board of Peace envoy Nickolay Mladenov condemned the escalation without naming Israel directly — *"Two days of strikes across Gaza have killed civilians and destroyed medical supplies that people depend on"* — a statement so carefully neutered it could have been written by a public relations firm.

July was the **deadliest month in Gaza this year**, with 152 people killed, as disease spreads through Gaza. The cumulative toll since the *"ceasefire"* began: 1,250 dead, more than 4,100 wounded. Overall, since Israel's genocidal campaign began on October 7, 2023, at least **73,341 Palestinians have been killed** and **174,086 wounded**. Approximately 90 percent of Gaza's civilian infrastructure has been destroyed. Reconstruction costs are estimated by the UN at $70 billion.

As we documented when [900 Palestinians had been killed under the same ](https://untelevised.media/articles/900-dead-since-the-ceasefire-israel-s-killing-spree-and-the-west-s-criminal-silence/)[*"ceasefire"*](https://untelevised.media/articles/900-dead-since-the-ceasefire-israel-s-killing-spree-and-the-west-s-criminal-silence/), the pattern is not new. What is new is the audacity of the propaganda accompanying it — a *"historic disarmament deal"* announced while the bodies are still being pulled from the rubble.

## The Machinery of Cover

Israel's killing during a *"ceasefire"* is not a failure of the deal. It is the deal. The ceasefire framework, as constructed, allows Israel to continue attacking while claiming legal cover — and allows the United States to claim it is pursuing peace while arming and diplomatically shielding the state doing the killing. It allows Israel to keep expanding the [*“Yellow-Line“*](https://untelevised.media/articles/israel-s-yellow-line-is-expanding-across-gaza-a-de-facto-reoccupation-is-underway/)* and *[Gaza keep shrinking](https://untelevised.media/articles/gaza-is-shrinking-from-the-ground-the-ceasefire-was-never-real/)[*.*](https://untelevised.media/articles/gaza-is-shrinking-from-the-ground-the-ceasefire-was-never-real/)

The Board of Peace, Trump's hand-picked body overseeing the process, has proven itself incapable of preventing a single civilian death. Mladenov's statement — condemning strikes that *"killed civilians and destroyed medical supplies"* without identifying the perpetrator — is a masterclass in the language of complicity dressed as concern.

Netanyahu's government has made its position explicit: Israeli forces will not withdraw until full disarmament. Hamas has made its position equally clear: no disarmament until Israel stops killing and leaves. The deadlock is the point. Israel does not want a resolution. It wants the pretense of one — enough cover to keep killing, keep building settlements in the West Bank, keep expanding the *"Yellow Line"* that carves occupied zones into what remains of Gaza.

## An Unborn Child Is Not Collateral Damage

There is a word for killing a pregnant woman and recovering her unborn child from the rubble of a destroyed apartment building. It is not *"tragic."* It is not *"complex."* It is not something that can be softened with quotation marks around the word ceasefire or hand-waved by an envoy who cannot bring himself to name the country doing the bombing.

The pattern is clear, and it has been clear for years. Israel kills Palestinians. The United States provides the weapons, the diplomatic cover, and the language of *"peace"* that makes the killing politically palatable. The media reports the announcements without reporting what those announcements conceal. The dead are buried, and the world moves on — until the next *"historic deal,"* and the next batch of names.
