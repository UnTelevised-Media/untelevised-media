## The Stand-Down Order

Perhaps the most damning detail of October 7 is what didn't happen. An Israeli soldier who survived the attack on Kibbutz Be'eri confirmed that troops stationed near the Gaza border were ordered early on the morning of October 7 to stay in their posts and not to patrol the fence for several hours — the precise hours during which Hamas fighters breached the perimeter.

When the attack began around 6:30 AM, the first order for available units to head south wasn't issued until 7:43 AM — more than an hour later. It wasn't until around noon that the IDF realized its Gaza Division — a force of just over 700 soldiers responsible for securing the entire Gaza border — had been completely overwhelmed. For nearly 10 hours, the Gaza Division was defeated and overrun, while military planners at the Kirya headquarters remained unaware of the full scope of the disaster.

## Insider Trading: Profits from Foreknowledge
