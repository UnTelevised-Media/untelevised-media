## Bab al-Mandab: The Chokepoint That Could Choke the Empire

But the real threat — the one that has Washington and Riyadh scrambling — came three days later from Mohammed al-Bukhaiti, a senior Ansarallah official. In an interview with Drop Site News, al-Bukhaiti warned that the strikes on Sanaa were *"merely one of the initial steps in a broader escalation."*

The centerpiece: Ansarallah would close the **Bab al-Mandab strait** — the narrow chokepoint connecting the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden and the Indian Ocean, through which much of Saudi Arabia's oil exports flow to Asian markets.

> *"We will focus on intensifying our military operations. The Bab al-Mandab strait is a legitimate leverage tool." — Mohammed al-Bukhaiti, Ansarallah official

## Who Are the Houthis? The Empire's Favorite Boogeyman

They are not a proxy. Their restraint during the Iran-Israel war — avoiding Red Sea shipping attacks despite pressure from Tehran — reflected strategic calculation, not weakness. They prioritized domestic consolidation. Now that consolidation has reached a point where they feel ready to escalate.
