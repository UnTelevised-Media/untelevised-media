## The Victims

Their names:

Aws Hamdi al-Naasan — 14 years old. Ninth grade student. Shot in the head while trying to escape his school. His friends carried his body away as bullets continued to fly.

Marzouq Abu Naim (also reported as Jihad Abu Naim) — 32 years old. Parent at the school. Lived nearby. Rushed to the school when he heard the gunshots. Shot dead while trying to save the children.

Four others were wounded — students and parents.

The attack happened around noon on April 21, 2026, at a school in Al-Mughayyir village, northeast of Ramallah in the occupied West Bank.

## What Happened

Bassam Abu-Assaf, the school principal, told reporters that at least five armed settlers approached the village around noon. Some students were outside in the schoolyard when the gunfire began.

Video from the scene shows a man in military fatigues carrying a rifle, advancing slowly toward the village before crouching and firing at least eight rounds directly at the school. Activists identified the man as a known settler who has attacked the village before — he has a track record of terrorizing Palestinians, and he's still free.

"I saw shooting directed at classroom windows and balconies still full of children," a witness told Drop Site News. "Residents were attempting to evacuate the school by crawling."

A paramedic at the scene said at least three settlers deliberately fired at children attempting to escape from classrooms, positioned about 50 meters away — what he described as close to sniping accuracy.

Israel Defense Forces arrived during the attack. According to witnesses, they didn't stop the settlers. They protected them.

A 63-year-old man, Attallah Abu Aliya, told Reuters he was shot in the leg by an Israeli soldier without warning as he walked toward the school after hearing it was under attack.

This is the pattern: settlers attack, soldiers arrive, Palestinians die.

## The Aftermath

Videos filmed outside the school show the streets splattered with blood. Men and boys, young and old, run frantically, calling for help. Injured children and adults are carried away — one man with an exposed, bloodied torso.

"It was a disaster. Everybody was screaming. It was unbelievable," Abu-Assaf said. "I'm still processing it. I don't know how long it will take for the teachers and students to overcome the shock."

The Israeli military's excuse: rocks were thrown at a vehicle carrying Israeli passengers, including a reserve soldier, who "exited the vehicle and opened fire at suspects in the area."

A military source told The Guardian that the reservist has been suspended from duty pending a criminal investigation.

But here's what happens to Israelis who kill Palestinians: nothing.

Israel has not prosecuted a single Israeli citizen for killing Palestinian civilians in the occupied West Bank since the start of this decade. That's not a typo. Zero prosecutions. While Israel's military claims to investigate, investigations go nowhere, perpetrators face no consequences, and the killing continues.

## The Death Toll

Since October 7, 2023, Israeli soldiers and settlers have killed at least 1,152 Palestinians in the West Bank — including 239 children. More than 11,800 have been injured.

In the West Bank alone, Israeli forces and settlers have killed at least 1,079 Palestinians, with at least 235 of them children, according to UNRWA.

These aren't combat deaths. These are civilians, many of them children, killed in their own communities, often in their own schools.

## Legal Context: This Is Illegal

Both Israel's occupation of Palestine and the colonization of Palestinian lands by Jewish settlers are illegal under international law. Article 49 of the Fourth Geneva Convention states that an "occupying power shall not deport or transfer parts of its own civilian population into the territory it occupies."

In July 2024, the International Court of Justice — where Israel is currently facing a genocide case for its war in Gaza — found that the occupation of Palestine is an illegal form of apartheid that must end as soon as possible. The ICJ also ruled that Israeli settler colonization of the West Bank amounts to annexation, which is a crime under international law.

Israel knows it's illegal. The world knows it's illegal. The settlers know it's illegal.
