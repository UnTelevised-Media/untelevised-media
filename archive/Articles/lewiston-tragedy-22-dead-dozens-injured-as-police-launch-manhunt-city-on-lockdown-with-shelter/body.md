### **Tragedy in Lewiston: 22 Dead, Dozens Wounded; City on Lockdown as Police Launch Manhunt, Imposing Shelter-in-Place Order**

Updated at: October 26th 12:42AM

Lewiston, Maine, was rocked by a horrifying mass shooting incident on the evening of October 25, leaving 22 people dead and dozens more injured. The gunman, whose identity remains unknown, opened fire at multiple locations across the city, including Schemengees Bar and Grille Restaurant, a Walmart store, and Sparetime Recreation, a bowling alley. Law enforcement sources have confirmed the grim death toll, emphasizing the devastating impact of the event on the community.

This precautionary measure was implemented to ensure the safety of the community, minimizing the risk of further harm amid the ongoing crisis. Meanwhile, law enforcement agencies launched an intensive manhunt, deploying significant resources and personnel to locate and apprehend the suspect swiftly. Armed with photographs and information about the suspect's appearance, authorities are working tirelessly, combing the area, and employing sophisticated techniques to track down the individual responsible for the horrific mass shooting. The community, gripped by fear and uncertainty, anxiously awaits updates, hoping for a swift resolution and an end to the threat looming over their city.

  - **Incident:** On the evening of October 25, 2023, an active shooter opened fire at multiple locations in Lewiston, Maine, including Schemengees Bar and Grille Restaurant, a Walmart store, and Sparetime Recreation, a bowling alley.

  - **Law Enforcement Response:** Law enforcement agencies, including the Lewiston Police Department, Androscoggin County Sheriff's Office, Maine State Police, and the FBI, have initiated a manhunt for the suspect. Police have released photos of the suspect and a vehicle of interest.
  - **Shelter-in-Place Order:** Authorities have issued a shelter-in-place order for residents in Lewiston and Auburn, urging them to stay inside their homes with doors locked. Businesses have been encouraged to close or lock down.
  - **Presidential Briefing:** President Joe Biden has been briefed on the situation and is receiving updates.
  - **Suspect:** Police named Robert Card, 40, as a person of interest in connection with the shootings at Schemengees Bar and Sparetime Recreation.
