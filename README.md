# Untelevised Media

> **Independent news, music, and culture. Stories the mainstream won't tell.**

Untelevised is a digital media platform dedicated to in-depth journalism, critical analysis, and underreported stories. We cover the news, music, and culture that shape our world—from breaking investigations to artist profiles—with editorial independence and reader-first integrity.

## Our Mission

We exist to challenge the narrative. Mainstream media has consolidated power, prioritizing corporate interests and spectacle over substance. **Untelevised** operates outside that system: editorially independent, reader-supported, and built by journalists and contributors who believe in accountability and truth.

The consolidation of media ownership has created a dangerous blind spot. Six corporations control over 90% of U.S. media. The result: stories that threaten advertisers get buried. International conflicts get simplified into two-dimensional narratives. Voices from marginalized communities are systematized out. And when criticism of the system itself becomes necessary, the system stays silent.

We're here because that needs to change. Untelevised isn't a reaction—it's an alternative infrastructure. We're building a news system where journalists own their work, readers fund the coverage they believe in, and editorial decisions are based on importance, not profit.

## Coverage Areas

We report on what matters. Our coverage includes:

- **Geopolitics & Conflict** — International coverage that goes beyond mainstream framing. We cover U.S. foreign policy, global power dynamics, and conflicts that receive inadequate coverage in American media.
- **Domestic Accountability** — Government oversight, corporate malfeasance, and institutional failure. Stories about power and who's abusing it.
- **Human Rights** — Refugee crises, labor conditions, police violence, and systematic inequality. The stories that are hard to report but impossible to ignore.
- **Technology & Power** — How big tech shapes our society, from algorithms to surveillance to labor practices. Critical analysis of who benefits from the tech economy.
- **Music & Artists** — Profiles, album reviews, and analysis. We cover the artists mainstream music media ignores and the politics behind the art.
- **Culture & Ideas** — Essays, analysis, and cultural criticism that connect art, politics, and social movements.

## What You Get

### Breaking News & Investigation
Deep-dive reporting on stories overlooked by mainstream outlets. We invest weeks and months—not hours—in the stories that matter. Political accountability, human rights, conflict coverage, and investigative pieces that require real journalism. The nuance corporate media abandoned long ago.

### Fact-Checking & Verified Context
Context matters more than speed. We provide:
- **Verified timelines** of major events with source documentation
- **Real-time fact-checking** of claims with methodological transparency
- **Historical background** that explains *why* things are happening now
- **Primary source access** — the documents and evidence, not just commentary about them

### Music & Culture Analysis
Beyond the celebrity gossip and entertainment press releases. We publish:
- **Artist profiles** that explore their work and their politics
- **Album reviews** that analyze craft, themes, and cultural impact
- **Cultural criticism** that connects art to the broader moment

### Trending & Discovery
Find what's important and what's rising. Our trending system shows you:
- **Most-read pieces** by topic (not by engagement-farming clicks)
- **Emerging stories** from our contributor network
- **Related coverage** that gives you the full picture

### Contributor Network
Untelevised isn't gatekept journalism. We believe expertise comes in many forms:
- **Open submissions** — Journalists, researchers, experts, and voices with real knowledge can pitch and publish
- **Fair compensation** — Contributors earn from their work. We don't treat writers as free content
- **Editorial support** — New contributors get mentorship and editing support
- **Audience connection** — Your byline is permanent. Build your audience. Own your work

Our contributor portal lets you manage pitches, drafts, publications, and earnings in one place.

### Curated Bookstore
Independent journalism needs independent publishers. Our bookstore features:
- **Research & analysis** — Books and reports that dive deeper into the stories we cover
- **Commissioned works** — Original writing from journalists and researchers in our network
- **Independent publishers** — Small presses and self-published authors doing important work
- **Direct support** — Your purchase goes directly to the author or publisher, not through Amazon's distribution model

## What Makes Untelevised Different

### Editorial Independence
We have no corporate ownership. No venture capital telling us to chase growth over quality. No advertisers demanding we avoid certain topics. Stories are chosen based on importance and merit, not what keeps people scrolling. When a story threatens no one's interests except those in power—that's when we know it's journalism worth doing.

### Transparency Over Spectacle
We disclose our sources, our reporting process, and our limitations. When we make mistakes—and we will—corrections are posted prominently with explanation. We don't hide behind a paywall of methodology; readers can understand how and why we reported something the way we did.

### Contributor-First Model
Mainstream platforms treat writers as content machines—unlimited labor for algorithmic benefit. We flip that. Contributors earn from subscriptions to their work and articles. Your piece isn't fuel for someone else's ad network; it's your professional work deserving professional compensation.

### Reader-Supported Journalism
No surveillance capitalism. We don't build profiles on you, sell your data, or use your reading habits to train algorithms. Your membership and purchases support journalism. That's the entire transaction. If we're not serving readers, we fail—not advertisers.

### Algorithmic Integrity
We don't have an algorithm optimizing for outrage and addiction. Articles are organized by topic, date, and importance. You can browse the news by area of interest. You can find trending pieces based on what readers are actually reading and sharing. No engagement maximization. No viral incentive. Just journalism.

### Global Perspective
American mainstream media is American-centric by default. We cover international stories with the depth they deserve. Conflicts in the Global South get real reporting, not 30-second takes. Global economics, migration, climate justice, and power dynamics are covered by journalists who understand the regions and the stakes.

## Our Principles

- **Truth over narrative** — We report what's true, not what's convenient
- **Accountability first** — We challenge power, whoever holds it
- **Workers belong in media** — Journalists should be paid fairly and own their work
- **Global vision** — International stories matter as much as domestic ones
- **Accessibility** — Critical journalism shouldn't be locked behind paywalls indefinitely
- **Corrections in public** — We fix mistakes openly, not quietly
- **No algorithms optimizing for addiction** — We trust readers to decide what matters

## How Untelevised Works

### For Readers

**Browse by topic.** Discover news, music, and cultural writing organized by area of interest and editorial importance. No algorithmic chaos—just journalism organized logically.

**Read in depth.** Full articles with proper reporting—investigated, fact-checked, contextualized. Articles that take 10 minutes or 30 minutes to read, depending on the story's complexity. No TL;DR reductions that sacrifice understanding.

**Find connections.** Timelines, fact-checks, and related articles that give you the full context. See how stories connect across beats and time.

**Join a community.** Comment on articles, share your perspective, and engage with journalists and other readers. This is discourse without the engagement algorithm distortion.

**Support journalism directly.** Subscribe to support individual journalists or the platform as a whole. Buy from our bookstore. Every dollar goes to creators and operations—no investor extraction.

### For Journalists & Contributors

**No gatekeeping.** Have expertise, reporting, or a story that matters? You can submit. We review on merit, not credentials or connections.

**Keep your work.** Your article is yours. Your byline is permanent. You build audience and reputation on Untelevised, not for Untelevised.

**Get paid.** Contributors earn from subscriptions to their work and individual article purchases. Editors earn from the platform. No "exposure" economics.

**Get mentorship.** New contributors work with editors to develop their craft and reporting skills.

**Pitch directly.** See a story that needs reporting? Pitch it. Fully formed ideas, early-stage concepts, breaking news—we want to hear it.
